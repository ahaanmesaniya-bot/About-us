About R7 Play

R7 Play is a free game app where you can play many fun games in one place. It is made by R7 Studio.

What's inside

  • Puzzle, block-building and many other kinds of games
  • New and trending games in one place
  • Save your favourite games
  • Jump back into recently played games instantly
  • Hindi and English languages, plus dark and light themes
  • No account needed - just open and play
